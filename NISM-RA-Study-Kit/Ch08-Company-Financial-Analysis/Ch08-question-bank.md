### Answers

- **C14-1: d**. Change in inventory (an expense line) = opening &minus; closing = &minus;35. So closing = 320 + 35 = 355.
- **C14-2: d**. Raw materials to sales: A fell from 35.0% to 33.0%; B stayed at 34.0%. A falling ratio points to higher selling prices. Faster sales growth can come from volume, so it proves nothing about price.
- **C14-3: b**. EBITDA = PBT + finance cost + D&A. A: 4,093.1 + 180.4 + 241.2 = 4,514.7, margin 49.6%. B: 2,929.6 + 195.2 + 218.6 = 3,343.4, margin 44.4%. Gap about 518 bps.
- **C14-4: b**. Debt &divide; assets in 2XX9: A 1,640.5 &divide; 4,687 = 35.0%; B 1,626.8 &divide; 4,067 = 40.0%. A has more debt in rupees only because it is bigger.
- **C14-5: d**. The book calls EBIT operating profit. EBIT = PBT + finance cost = 2,929.6 + 195.2 = 3,124.8. (3,343.4 is EBITDA.)
- **C14-6: d**. 2XX9: margin A 30.4% vs B 26.1%; asset turnover A 1.94 vs B 1.85; equity multiplier A 1.54 vs B 1.67. A's lower leverage pulls its ROE down, so it is not a favourable factor.
