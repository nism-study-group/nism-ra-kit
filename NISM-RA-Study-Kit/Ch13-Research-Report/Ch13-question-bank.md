### Answers

- **Q13.1: d**. Book sample question. The book names annual reports, quarterly reports and calculations as the sources for the fact-based sections.
- **Q13.2: d**. Book sample question. D/E and interest coverage are in the quantitative part of the checklist.
- **Q13.3: c**. Book sample question. The valuation parameters include intrinsic value, P/E and P/BV.
- **Q13.4: b**. All analysts have more or less the same information.
- **Q13.5: c**. Unnecessary details are a reason reports fail.
- **Q13.6: d**. As in any writing project.
- **Q13.7: c**. View-based: company business, key strengths, key concerns, industry overview.
- **Q13.8: b**. Fact-based sections come from annual and quarterly reports and calculations.
- **Q13.9: b**. The other three are on the book's list of communications excluded from the definition of research report.
- **Q13.10: c**. They are on the book's list of exclusions.
- **Q13.11: a**. The book recommends charts: visual data is easier to understand.
- **Q13.12: d**. Buy: more than 10%. Sell: less than &minus;10%.
- **Q13.13: c**. Overweight means expected to do better than the market.
- **Q13.14: a**. The analyst may even indicate the expected level of out- or underperformance.
- **Q13.15: b**. Errors of ineptitude come from incorrect use of knowledge we have.
- **Q13.16: a**. No method guarantees profits.
- **Q13.17: c**. The book's thresholds are indicative only.
- **Q13.18: a**. From the quantitative parameters.
- **Q13.19: a**. It tests whether the business is simple, the output a necessity and competition not tough.
- **Q13.20: d**. Along with intrinsic value and relative cheapness.

## Case C25: Ritu's first report

Ritu is writing her first research report on a cement company during results season. Her draft has long paragraphs, detailed plant-by-plant tables nobody asked for, and a 'buy' call on page 2 that turns into 'cautious' by page 9. She plans to fill the shareholding and peer tables from annual reports, and the strengths section from her meetings with management.

**C25-1.** Which reasons for report failure does her draft show?  
a) Unnecessary details, long sentences and inconsistent views  
b) Too many charts  
c) Too few sections  
d) Complex visuals and short sentences  

**C25-2.** Filling the shareholding and peer tables from annual reports is correct because they are:  
a) Fact-based sections  
b) Valuation sections  
c) Optional sections  
d) View-based sections  

**C25-3.** Writing the strengths section from management meetings is correct because it is:  
a) A fact-based section  
b) A statistical summary  
c) Not part of a research report  
d) A view-based section  

**C25-4.** Ritu's firm defines 'buy' as more than 10% return in 12 months. If she expects 4%, the book's example scale suggests:  
a) Overweight  
b) Sell  
c) Buy  
d) Hold  

### Answers

- **C25-1: a**. These are three of the book's five reasons for failure.
- **C25-2: a**. Fact-based sections come from annual and quarterly reports and calculations.
- **C25-3: d**. View-based sections draw on management communication and personal understanding.
- **C25-4: d**. Hold is between &minus;10% and 10% in the book's example.

## Case C26: Checking Delta Paints against the checklist

Over the last 5 years, Delta Paints has had a net profit margin of 12% to 14%, with EPS rising every year. Its D/E has been between 0.3 and 0.5 and interest coverage above 8. ROE has ranged from 18% to 22% and ROCE from 20% to 25%. It paid a dividend every year. One year, operating cash flow was negative.

**C26-1.** Does Delta pass the book's profitability test?  
a) It cannot be judged  
b) No: EPS must be flat  
c) Yes: NPM above 10% with EPS growing every year  
d) No: NPM must be above 20%  

**C26-2.** Does Delta pass the book's leverage test?  
a) No: interest coverage must be below 3  
b) No: D/E must be zero  
c) It cannot be judged  
d) Yes: D/E below 1 and interest coverage above 3  

**C26-3.** Which checklist test does Delta fail?  
a) Return test  
b) Positive operating cash flow every year for 5 years  
c) Dividend record  
d) Leverage test  

**C26-4.** These tests belong to which part of the checklist?  
a) Rating conventions  
b) Quantitative parameters  
c) Qualitative parameters  
d) Valuation parameters  

### Answers

- **C26-1: c**. The book's test: NPM above 10% and EPS growth continuously over 5 years.
- **C26-2: d**. D/E below 1 and interest coverage above 3, continuously.
- **C26-3: b**. The book asks for positive operating and free cash flows continuously over 5 years.
- **C26-4: b**. They are financial track-record tests.
