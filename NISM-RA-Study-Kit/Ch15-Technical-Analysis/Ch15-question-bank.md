## Case C30: Kabir reads the candles

Kabir watches Alpha Ltd. After falling for six sessions, Alpha forms a candle with a small body near the day's high and a lower shadow three times the body. Months later, near the top of a long trading range, a strong green candle is followed by a candle that opens with a gap up but closes red, below the midpoint of the green candle.

**C30-1.** The candle after the six-session fall is most likely a:  
a) Hammer  
b) Hanging man  
c) Dark cloud cover  
d) Evening star  

**C30-2.** What does Kabir need before acting on it?  
a) Confirmation: price must start moving up next  
b) Nothing; act immediately  
c) A gap down the next day  
d) RSI above 70  

**C30-3.** The gap-up red candle closing below the green candle's midpoint, at the top of a range, is:  
a) Piercing pattern  
b) Bullish engulfing  
c) Dark cloud cover  
d) Morning star  

**C30-4.** If the same shape as the first candle had appeared after a strong rise, it would be a:  
a) Hanging man, a bearish warning  
b) Hammer, bullish  
c) Bullish engulfing  
d) Morning star  

### Answers

- **C30-1: a**. Small body, long lower shadow at least twice the body, after a decline.
- **C30-2: a**. The book says trades are typically taken after the confirmation candle.
- **C30-3: c**. A bearish two-candle pattern near the top of a congestion area.
- **C30-4: a**. The hanging man needs the next candle to close lower to confirm.

## Case C31: A triangle on Beta Ltd

Beta Ltd is in an uptrend. For two months it has hit Rs 600 four times and fallen back, while each dip has stopped higher than the last: 540, 560, 575. Today it closed at Rs 612 on volume twice the average. The pattern's height at its thickest point is Rs 60.

**C31-1.** What pattern has Beta formed?  
a) Ascending triangle  
b) Symmetrical triangle  
c) Flag  
d) Descending triangle  

**C31-2.** What is the book's price target from the breakout at Rs 600?  
a) Rs 540  
b) Rs 660  
c) Rs 612  
d) Rs 720  

**C31-3.** Why does the high volume matter?  
a) High volume always signals a false breakout  
b) Volume confirms the breakout and adds credibility  
c) Volume is irrelevant to breakouts  
d) It means the stock is oversold  

**C31-4.** If price later falls back to Rs 600, the old resistance would be expected to act as:  
a) Resistance  
b) A flagpole  
c) A pivot for selling only  
d) Support  

### Answers

- **C31-1: a**. Flat resistance at 600 with higher lows.
- **C31-2: b**. Height at the thickest point (60) added to the breakout point (600).
- **C31-3: b**. High volume during breakouts adds credibility to the move.
- **C31-4: d**. Role reversal: broken resistance often becomes support.

## Case C32: Indicators on Gamma Ltd

Gamma Ltd's price made a new high this month, but its RSI peaked lower than last month and its MACD fast line made a lower high. RSI had been above 75 for six weeks. ADX is 18 and falling. Over five years, Gamma's RSC against the Nifty 50 has been below 100, but it just crossed above 100.

**C32-1.** Price makes a new high while RSI does not. This is:  
a) A confirmed uptrend  
b) Irrelevant in an overbought market  
c) Bearish divergence, an early sell signal  
d) Bullish divergence  

**C32-2.** What does the ADX reading of 18 and falling tell the analyst?  
a) The stock is oversold  
b) The price will fall  
c) The trend is strong and rising  
d) The trend is weak; DMI crossovers should be avoided  

**C32-3.** The MACD lower high while price makes a higher high means:  
a) The zero line has been crossed  
b) Momentum is accelerating  
c) The stock is losing upward momentum  
d) A buy signal  

**C32-4.** What does the RSC crossing above 100 after a long time below suggest, as per the book?  
a) The stock is overbought  
b) The stock may now be a good buy relative to the benchmark  
c) The stock is underperforming  
d) Nothing; RSC is only for commodities  

### Answers

- **C32-1: c**. In an overbought market, a new price high without a new RSI high is bearish divergence.
- **C32-2: d**. ADX below 25 is weak or no trend; it says nothing about direction.
- **C32-3: c**. A negative divergence between price and MACD.
- **C32-4: b**. The book: a stock becomes a good buy if RSC stays below 100 for long, then turns up and crosses 100. The signals conflict, so more confirmation is needed.
