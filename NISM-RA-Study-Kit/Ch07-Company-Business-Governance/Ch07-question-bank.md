## Case C12: SWOT for Kiran Paints

Kiran Paints is a mid-sized paint company. It has no debt and a well-known brand. Two dealers account for 45% of its sales. The government is expected to tighten rules on chemicals used in paints. A large competitor has just exited the market after losses. Kiran's management has never run a factory outside India.

**C12-1.** Two dealers accounting for 45% of sales is a:  
a) Threat  
b) Weakness  
c) Opportunity  
d) Strength  

**C12-2.** The large competitor exiting the market is best classed as a(n):  
a) Opportunity  
b) Strength  
c) Threat  
d) Weakness  

**C12-3.** Tighter rules on chemicals in paints are a:  
a) Opportunity  
b) Weakness  
c) Strength  
d) Threat  

**C12-4.** An equity analyst studying Kiran should, as per the book, start the SWOT with:  
a) Strengths and weaknesses  
b) Only weaknesses  
c) Opportunities and threats  
d) Only strengths  

### Answers

- **C12-1: b**. High customer concentration is internal, so it is a weakness.
- **C12-2: a**. An external event that opens room to gain share, like Jet Airways' exit in the book.
- **C12-3: d**. A regulatory headwind from the external environment.
- **C12-4: c**. The second approach suits outside analysts and follows the E-I-C order.

## Case C13: Governance check at Orbit Logistics

Orbit Logistics has 9 directors. Its chairman is also its managing director. 3 directors are independent. The audit committee has 3 members, of whom 2 are independent. The same audit firm has audited Orbit for 8 years, and Orbit's fees are 15% of the audit firm's total income. Promoters have pledged most of their shares.

**C13-1.** Does the board meet the book's SEBI requirement for independent directors?  
a) No. With an executive chairman, at least 50% must be independent, but only one third are  
b) Yes. One third independent is enough in all cases  
c) No. All directors must be independent  
d) Yes. Any number above two is enough  

**C13-2.** Does the audit committee meet the book's SEBI requirement?  
a) No. All members must be independent by law  
b) No. At least 50% must be executive directors  
c) No. The committee must have at least 9 members  
d) Yes. Two of three is two thirds independent  

**C13-3.** Which auditor-related concern does the book's checklist raise here?  
a) Only the rotation  
b) Only the fees  
c) Neither  
d) Both: fees above 10% of the auditor's income, and no rotation in five years  

**C13-4.** What is the main risk from the promoters' heavy pledge?  
a) Promoters lose voting rights immediately  
b) The auditor must resign  
c) A price fall could force lenders to sell, pushing the price down further  
d) The company cannot pay dividends  

### Answers

- **C13-1: a**. With an executive chairman the book says at least 50%. 3 of 9 is one third.
- **C13-2: d**. SEBI requires at least two thirds; ideally all would be independent.
- **C13-3: d**. Ideal: fees below 10% of income and rotation once in five years.
- **C13-4: c**. High pledges aggravate market risk through forced liquidation.
