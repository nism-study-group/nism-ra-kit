# Session 0: Orientation: the exam, the book and the plan

Question bank for the NISM Series XV study group. Answers and explanations are at the end of each section so you can test yourself first.

Scoring in the exam: +1 right, minus 0.25 wrong, 0 skipped.

## Multiple-choice questions

**Q0.1.** The NISM Series XV exam has ______ questions to be answered in ______.  
a) 80; 2 hours  
b) 100; 3 hours  
c) 120; 2 hours  
d) 100; 2 hours  

**Q0.2.** Which of the following best describes the structure of the paper?  
a) 70 MCQs of 1 mark and 10 case studies with 3 questions each  
b) 80 MCQs of 1 mark and 5 case studies with 4 questions of 1 mark each  
c) 80 MCQs of 1 mark and 10 case studies with 2 questions each  
d) 100 MCQs of 1 mark each  

**Q0.3.** What is the passing score?  
a) 75 marks  
b) 60 marks

**Q0.4.** For a 1-mark question answered wrongly, the candidate loses:  
a) 0.25 marks  
b) 0.5 marks

**Q0.5.** A candidate gets 64 right, 24 wrong and skips 12. What is the net score?  
a) 60  
b) 64  
c) 52

**Q0.6.** Which chapter carries the highest weight in the exam?  
a) Company Analysis: Financial Analysis  
b) Technical Analysis  
c) Legal and Regulatory Environment

**Q0.7.** Chapters 1, 2 and 3 together carry ______ marks.  
a) 10  
b) 3  
c) 5

**Q0.8.** Which software will be available on the test centre computers for numericals?  
a) Microsoft Excel or LibreOffice Calc

**Q0.9.** The passing certificate is issued only to candidates who have:

**Q0.10.** Passing this exam is a requirement under which provision?  
a) The SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013

**Q0.11.** The February 2026 workbook applies to exams taken on or after:  
a) 1 April 2026  
b) 1 February 2026  
c) 20 January 2026

**Q0.12.** On a 4-option question, you can rule out two options. On average, answering gains you about:  
a) +0.17 marks  
b) +0.06 marks  
c) +0.38 marks  
d) Nothing; it is better to skip  

**Q0.13.** Which statement about passing the exam is correct?  
a) It is valid for life  
b) It does not by itself register the person with SEBI as a research analyst  
c) It registers the person with SEBI automatically  
d) It is needed only by independent analysts, not employees  

**Q0.14.** Chapters 8, 10, 14 and 15 together carry:  
a) 49 marks  
b) 39 marks  
c) 25 marks  
d) 54 marks  

### Answers

- **Q0.1: d**. 100 questions of 1 mark each, in 2 hours (120 minutes).
- **Q0.2: b**. 80 standalone MCQs (80 marks) plus 5 cases &times; 4 questions (20 marks).
- **Q0.3: b**. The pass mark is 60 out of 100, that is 60%.
- **Q0.4: a**. Negative marking is 25% of the marks for the question, so 0.25 for a 1-mark question.
- **Q0.5: d**. 64 minus 24 &times; 0.25 = 64 minus 6 = 58. That is a fail, even though 64 answers were right.
- **Q0.6: b**. Technical Analysis carries 15 marks. Financial Analysis and Valuation carry 12 each; Legal and Regulatory carries 10.
- **Q0.7: c**. 1 + 2 + 2 = 5 marks.
- **Q0.8: a**. The workbook says the workstations have either Microsoft Excel or LibreOffice Calc, and advises being comfortable with both.
- **Q0.9: a**. NISM issues the certificate only if the PAN is in the registration details.
- **Q0.10: c**. The workbook states that associated persons must pass the exam to meet Regulation 7(2) of the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.
- **Q0.11: d**. 30 March 2026. The tempting wrong answer is 20 January 2026, which is when the revised exam pattern launched.
- **Q0.12: c**. A 1 in 2 chance: 0.5 &times; 1 minus 0.5 &times; 0.25 = 0.375. +0.06 is a blind guess; +0.17 is after ruling out one.
- **Q0.13: b**. Passing gives a certificate valid for 3 years. SEBI registration is a separate process, and the exam applies to employees and partners too.
- **Q0.14: a**. 12 + 12 + 10 + 15 = 49 marks, nearly half the paper.

## Case C0: Meera's first mock test

Meera takes a full 100-question mock test under exam rules. She answers 70 questions and skips 30. Of the 70 she answered, 58 are right and 12 are wrong.

**C0-1.** What is Meera's net score?  
a) 55  
b) 46  
c) 61  
d) 58  

**C0-2.** Did Meera pass?  
a) Yes, because she answered 70 questions  
b) No, she is 2 marks short  
c) Yes, she passed with 58  
d) No, she is 5 marks short  

**C0-3.** Keeping her 12 wrong answers the same, how many of the 30 skipped questions must she answer correctly to pass?  
a) 3  
b) 12  
c) 5  
d) 2  

**C0-4.** In 20 of the skipped questions, Meera could have ruled out two options each. If she had guessed on all 20, how much would she have gained on average?  
a) Nothing  
b) About 7.5 marks  
c) About 3.4 marks  
d) About 1.2 marks  

### Answers

- **C0-1: a**. 58 minus 12 &times; 0.25 = 58 minus 3 = 55.
- **C0-2: d**. The pass mark is 60 and her net score is 55, so she is 5 marks short. 58 is her count of right answers, not her score.
- **C0-3: c**. Each extra right answer adds 1 mark. 55 + 5 = 60.
- **C0-4: b**. Each such guess is worth 0.5 &times; 1 minus 0.5 &times; 0.25 = 0.375 on average. 20 &times; 0.375 = 7.5, enough to pass on average.
