## Case C17: Meghna's corporate action year

Meghna holds 600 shares of Sunita Steel, face value Rs 10, trading at Rs 900. During the year the company: (1) announces a 1:2 bonus issue; (2) later announces a stock split of 1:2; (3) finally offers rights in the ratio 1-for-6 at a discount to market price.

**C17-1.** How many shares does Meghna hold after the bonus issue?  
a) 800  
b) 900  
c) 1,200  
d) 300  

**C17-2.** Ignoring market movements, what is the theoretical price after the bonus?  
a) Rs 300  
b) Rs 900  
c) Rs 600  
d) Rs 450  

**C17-3.** After the 1:2 split, what are Meghna's shares and face value?  
a) 1,800 shares of Rs 10  
b) 1,800 shares of Rs 5  
c) 900 shares of Rs 20  
d) 450 shares of Rs 5  

**C17-4.** Meghna does not want to invest more in the rights issue. Which statement is correct?  
a) She will receive the rights shares free  
b) Her ownership share is unaffected whatever she does  
c) She can renounce her rights to someone else; if she does nothing, her ownership share is diluted  
d) She must subscribe, as rights are compulsory  

### Answers

- **C17-1: b**. 1 bonus share for every 2 held: 600 &divide; 2 = 300 new. Total 900.
- **C17-2: c**. Value stays 600 &times; 900 = 5,40,000. Spread over 900 shares: Rs 600.
- **C17-3: b**. Each share becomes 2 and face value halves: 900 &times; 2 = 1,800 shares of Rs 5.
- **C17-4: c**. Subscribing is a choice. Dilution hits only those who do not take up their rights.

## Case C18: Orion Pharma wants to leave the exchange

Orion Pharma's promoters hold 78% of its shares. They plan to delist voluntarily. Orion has spare cash and also considers a buyback first. Orion had once been delisted compulsorily years ago and later relisted.

**C18-1.** Which method must the promoters use to acquire public shares for voluntary delisting?  
a) Open market purchase only  
b) A bonus issue  
c) A rights issue at a discount  
d) Reverse book building with a floor price  

**C18-2.** Under the book's conditions, when can the delisting succeed?  
a) Whenever SEBI approves, whatever the holding  
b) As soon as promoter holding crosses 75%  
c) Only if all public shareholders sell  
d) Only if promoter holding crosses 90% and at least 25% of public shareholders participate  

**C18-3.** If Orion does a buyback first, which statement is correct?  
a) It can use only reserves and surplus, and the shares bought back are extinguished  
b) It needs only a board resolution  
c) It increases the number of shares outstanding  
d) It can borrow to fund it and keep the shares as treasury stock  

**C18-4.** After Orion's earlier compulsory delisting, how long did it have to wait to apply for relisting, as per the book?  
a) 10 years  
b) 3 years  
c) 5 years  
d) 1 year  

### Answers

- **C18-1: d**. Shareholders bid the price at which they will sell, above the promoters' floor price.
- **C18-2: d**. Both conditions apply. No minority shareholder can be forced to exit.
- **C18-3: a**. A special resolution with the time frame and maximum price is also needed.
- **C18-4: a**. 5 years after voluntary delisting, 10 years after compulsory delisting.
