## Case C27: Priyanka's busy month

Priyanka is an analyst at a research entity that also has an investment banking division. On 1 June she starts preparing a buy report on Zeta Ltd, published on 20 June. Her firm co-managed Zeta's IPO, priced on 5 June. Her bonus this year is proposed to include a share of the IPO fee. She owns 1.2% of Zeta.

**C27-1.** Under the RA Regulations, when can Priyanka not trade in Zeta's shares?  
a) From 30 days before to 5 days after publication on 20 June  
b) She can trade at any time  
c) Only on 20 June  
d) Only for 5 days before publication  

**C27-2.** Since her firm co-managed the IPO priced on 5 June, the earliest it may publish without special approval is:  
a) 25 days after 5 June  
b) Immediately  
c) 10 days after 5 June  
d) 40 days after 5 June  

**C27-3.** The proposed bonus is:  
a) Allowed after SEBI approval  
b) Allowed if disclosed  
c) Not allowed, as pay cannot be based on a specific investment banking transaction  
d) Allowed if under 1% of the fee  

**C27-4.** What must the report disclose about her 1.2% holding?  
a) That she owns 1% or more of Zeta's securities  
b) Only if she sells  
c) Nothing, as it is under 5%  
d) Only her purchase price  

### Answers

- **C27-1: a**. Regulation 16(2): 30 days before and 5 days after publication.
- **C27-2: d**. 40 days after pricing for an IPO; the 20 June date is too early unless legal or compliance staff give prior written approval.
- **C27-3: c**. Regulation 17(1).
- **C27-4: a**. Regulation 19 requires disclosure of ownership of 1% or more.

## Case C28: A tip at the dinner table

Arun's brother-in-law is the CFO of Omega Ltd. At dinner, he mentions that Omega will announce a merger next week and that the auditors resigned yesterday. Arun runs a small research firm and plans to send a 'buy Omega' message to all his clients the next morning, and to buy for himself first.

**C28-1.** The merger news and the auditor's resignation are best described as:  
a) Mosaic analysis  
b) Public information  
c) Market commentary  
d) Unpublished price sensitive information  

**C28-2.** As the CFO's relative who has received UPSI, Arun is:  
a) Free to trade, as he is not an employee  
b) An insider, and must not trade while in possession of it  
c) Only barred from selling  
d) Allowed to trade if he informs the exchange  

**C28-3.** Buying for himself before sending the recommendation to clients would also breach the code of conduct on:  
a) None, it is allowed  
b) Front running  
c) Professional standard only  
d) Diligence only  

**C28-4.** Under which section of the SEBI Act were the PIT Regulations made?  
a) Section 11(2) only  
b) Section 30  
c) Section 3  
d) Section 12  

### Answers

- **C28-1: d**. Both are on the UPSI list: mergers, and resignation of a statutory auditor.
- **C28-2: b**. An insider includes anyone in possession of UPSI; relatives of connected persons are deemed connected.
- **C28-3: b**. The code bars insider trading and front running, including of one's own report.
- **C28-4: b**. Both PIT 2015 and PFUTP 2003 were made under section 30.

## Case C29: Sunil's social media launch

Sunil, an individual SEBI-registered research analyst, plans an Instagram campaign: "India's No. 1 research analyst. 90% accuracy guaranteed. Last year our picks returned 45%. Free trial!" (the trial needs a paid sign-up). He also wants to sell mutual funds to his research clients and to use the SEBI logo for credibility.

**C29-1.** Which part of the ad is allowed?  
a) "90% accuracy guaranteed"  
b) "No. 1 research analyst"  
c) "Last year our picks returned 45%"  
d) None of the quoted claims; each one breaks the advertisement code  

**C29-2.** Can Sunil use the SEBI logo?  
a) Only on his website  
b) Yes, as he is registered  
c) No, advertisements shall not include the SEBI logo  
d) Yes, with SEBI's email approval  

**C29-3.** Can Sunil sell mutual funds to his research clients?  
a) Yes, for clients who agree in writing  
b) Yes, if he discloses it  
c) No, an individual RA shall not provide distribution services  
d) Only debt funds  

**C29-4.** What standard warning must the ad carry?  
a) "Investment in securities market is subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing."  
b) No warning is needed on social media  
c) "Past performance guarantees future returns."  
d) "Approved by SEBI."  

### Answers

- **C29-1: d**. Superlatives, percentage accuracy or assured returns, past performance and "free" when not free are all prohibited.
- **C29-2: c**. The SEBI logo must not be used by IAs or RAs.
- **C29-3: c**. Regulation 26C.
- **C29-4: a**. Unchanged, in at least 10-point font, and accurately translated if not in English.
