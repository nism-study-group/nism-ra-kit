## Case C23: Comparing two fund managers

Fund P returned 16% a year with a standard deviation of 20% and a beta of 1.25. Fund Q returned 13% with a standard deviation of 10% and a beta of 0.8. The risk-free rate is 6% and the market risk premium is 6%. Neha, the investor, holds a broadly diversified portfolio across asset classes.

**C23-1.** Which fund has the higher Sharpe ratio?  
a) Fund P: 8.0 versus 8.75  
b) Fund P: 0.50 versus 0.70  
c) Fund Q: 0.70 versus 0.50  
d) Both are equal  

**C23-2.** What are the Treynor ratios?  
a) P 10; Q 7  
b) P 12.8; Q 10.4  
c) P 0.50; Q 0.70  
d) P 8.0; Q 8.75  

**C23-3.** What is Fund P's Jensen's alpha?  
a) 10%  
b) 1.0%  
c) negative 2.5%  
d) 2.5%  

**C23-4.** Neha is well diversified. As per the book, which ratio suits her best?  
a) Sharpe ratio  
b) Holding period return  
c) Treynor ratio  
d) Standard deviation  

### Answers

- **C23-1: c**. P: (16 &minus; 6) &divide; 20 = 0.50. Q: (13 &minus; 6) &divide; 10 = 0.70.
- **C23-2: d**. P: 10 &divide; 1.25 = 8.0. Q: 7 &divide; 0.8 = 8.75.
- **C23-3: d**. CAPM return = 6 + 1.25 &times; 6 = 13.5%. Alpha = 16 &minus; 13.5 = 2.5%.
- **C23-4: c**. The book says Treynor suits investors who have adequately diversified; Sharpe suits those who have not.

## Case C24: Raman's investment diary

Raman bought shares for Rs 50,000 and sold them 2 years later for Rs 60,500, with no dividends. He keeps his savings in bank deposits too. When a stock he owns falls 30%, he refuses to sell until it returns to his purchase price, and he buys stocks mainly after seeing friends make money on them.

**C24-1.** What is Raman's CAGR on the shares?  
a) 10%  
b) 5%  
c) 21%  
d) 10.5%  

**C24-2.** What is his simple (holding period) return?  
a) 10%  
b) 21%  
c) 10.5%  
d) 42%  

**C24-3.** Refusing to sell until the price returns to his purchase price shows:  
a) Anchoring  
b) Herd mentality  
c) Gambler's fallacy  
d) Winner's curse  

**C24-4.** Buying stocks after seeing friends make money shows:  
a) Confirmation bias  
b) Herd mentality  
c) Ownership bias  
d) Projection bias  

### Answers

- **C24-1: a**. (60,500 &divide; 50,000)1/2 &minus; 1 = 1.210.5 &minus; 1 = 10%.
- **C24-2: b**. 60,500 &divide; 50,000 &minus; 1 = 21%. Simple annualised would be 10.5%; CAGR is 10%.
- **C24-3: a**. He anchors to his first price and ignores new information.
- **C24-4: b**. Following others in the belief they know more.
