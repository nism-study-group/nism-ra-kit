## Case C8: Reading the economy for a housing finance company

Arjun covers a housing finance company. This year: inflation has risen sharply, the central bank has raised the repo rate twice, the government has announced higher spending on roads with lower income taxes, and FPIs have sold heavily while FDI inflows are steady.

**C8-1.** The central bank raising the repo rate is an example of:  
a) Contractionary monetary policy  
b) Expansionary fiscal policy  
c) Neutral fiscal policy  
d) Expansionary monetary policy  

**C8-2.** Higher spending with lower taxes is likely to:  
a) Create a fiscal surplus  
b) Be called contractionary fiscal policy  
c) Widen the fiscal deficit  
d) Reduce money with citizens  

**C8-3.** Why should Arjun worry about higher rates for his company?  
a) Higher rates always increase home loan demand  
b) Rates affect only export businesses  
c) Real estate is not affected by interest rates  
d) Home buying depends heavily on loans, so demand may fall  

**C8-4.** Which foreign flow is described in the book as long term and stable?  
a) FPI  
b) Hot money  
c) Portfolio debt flows  
d) FDI  

### Answers

- **C8-1: a**. Raising rates to cool inflation is contractionary monetary policy.
- **C8-2: c**. This is expansionary fiscal policy, which results in a deficit.
- **C8-3: d**. The book says higher rates hit real estate and autos most, since buyers borrow.
- **C8-4: d**. FPI is hot money; FDI is long term and stable.

## Case C9: The umbrella maker's quarter

RainSafe Ltd sells umbrellas. Its sales were Rs 20 crore in April to June last year, Rs 12 crore in January to March this year, and Rs 24 crore in April to June this year. Separately, the steel it uses has become costlier because many mills shut down after a period of low prices.

**C9-1.** What is the year-over-year sales growth for April to June?  
a) 20%  
b) 4%  
c) 12%  
d) 100%  

**C9-2.** RainSafe's quarterly pattern is an example of a:  
a) Economic cycle  
b) Secular trend  
c) Seasonal trend  
d) Inventory cycle  

**C9-3.** Mills shutting after low prices, then prices rising, describes the:  
a) Secular trend  
b) Commodity cycle  
c) Theory of the firm  
d) Seasonal trend  

**C9-4.** Higher steel costs pushing up umbrella prices would be:  
a) Deflation  
b) Cost-push inflation  
c) A fall in NFIA  
d) Demand-pull inflation  

### Answers

- **C9-1: a**. (24 &minus; 20) &divide; 20 = 20%. Comparing with January to March (100%) would mostly show the season.
- **C9-2: c**. A predictable yearly pattern tied to weather.
- **C9-3: b**. High-cost suppliers abandon capacity when prices fall; lower supply lifts prices again.
- **C9-4: b**. Rising input costs cause cost-push inflation.
