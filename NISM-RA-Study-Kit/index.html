The paper has 80 standalone MCQs of one mark each, plus 5 case studies with 4 questions each. This pattern started on 20 January 2026. The test computer has Excel or LibreOffice Calc for numericals, so practise on both.

Should you guess?With 4 options, a blind guess earns on average 0.25 × 1 minus 0.75 × 0.25 = +0.06 marks. Slightly positive, but a coin toss. If you can rule out one option, the average rises to +0.17. Rule out two and it is +0.38. So: skip only when you have no idea at all. If you can cross out even one option, answer.
